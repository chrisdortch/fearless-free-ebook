About the book

A boy divided by light and darkness must learn what balance really costs.

On the island of Solumbra, Daemond Kalei'i inherits a world divided by prophecy, grief, old families, hidden maps, buried keys, and ancient forces. Fearless is not a simple war between good and evil. It is a story about the courage to stand in the middle long enough to see clearly.

The book is offered freely because the message matters: fearlessness is not the absence of fear. It is the decision to move through it with love, wisdom, patience, courage, and balance.

“The gray is not a lack of clarity. It is the only place where reality truly lives.”
☉

Light creates

Light reveals, warms, and gives motion. It is not always gentle; it can blind as easily as it guides.

◌

All things change

Transformation is the dance between form and ending. Nothing living remains fixed.

●

Darkness returns

Darkness protects, hides, cools, and completes. It is not evil; it is the space where truth waits.

Read the opening idea

“The sun does not simply switch on like a lamp; it bleeds into the sky, dragging a spectrum of bruised purples and hesitant golds across the horizon. Yet, we are taught to ignore the bleed. We are taught to wait for the binary.”

That is the heartbeat of the first book: stop reducing existence into easy opposites. Look harder. Stay longer. See the gray.

About RollinD

A story offered freely because the truth inside it should move.

RollinD writes at the intersection of myth, moral philosophy, fantasy, and symbolic storytelling. Fearless is built to be read as an adventure, but also as a compass: a way to think about courage, grief, cooperation, and growth without hiding inside false binaries.

FAQ

Everything needed to start reading.

Is the book really free?

Yes. The ebook is free to download in PDF and EPUB. There is no paywall and no signup requirement.

Which format should I choose?

Use PDF for the designed illustrated layout. Use EPUB for ebook apps and devices that support fixed-layout EPUB files.

Can I listen to the album?

Yes. The album section plays the public Suno playlist and includes direct MP3 links for each track.

Can I download the artwork?

Yes. The artwork section includes a free image collection download with the full-resolution book images.

Can I share it?

Yes. Share the website or the free ebook download links. Please keep the author credit intact.

Will there be more books?

The intended series is five books. Book I is the beginning: the altar opens, and the paths of light and darkness begin to depart.

Can this site be deployed on Vercel?

Yes. This is a standalone static site. Upload it to a new GitHub repository and import that new repository into Vercel as a new project.

Does this affect any existing project?

No. The package is self-contained and does not reference or modify any existing Vercel or GitHub project.
